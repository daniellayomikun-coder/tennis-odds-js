How the numbers work

Only ATP 1000, WTA 1000 and ATP Challenger singles are shown. Pinnacle often posts a game handicap and a total-games line but no moneyline. We read those lines, strip the bookmaker margin, and fit the serve strength of both players that reproduces them. That gives a win probability, fair moneyline odds, an estimated bookmaker moneyline, and a predicted total games and handicap. When Pinnacle does post a real moneyline, we show it next to the model.

Fair odds

Fair odds are just 100 divided by the win chance. A 50% chance is fair odds of 2.00: bet 1 and get 2 back, on average, with no house edge. Real bookmakers pay less than fair odds.

Rollovers

Rolling a bet means putting the whole return on the next pick. The payout multiplies fast, but the chance of survival shrinks just as fast, and at fair odds the two cancel out.

Accumulators

An accumulator needs every leg to land. Chances multiply, so four picks at 80% each only land about 41% of the time.
